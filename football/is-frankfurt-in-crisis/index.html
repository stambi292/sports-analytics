What Frankfurt can do

Three changes the numbers point to, in order of how much they are worth.

+6 pts

Turn high-scoring draws into wins

All three draws (3-3, 2-2, 2-2) came in games where Frankfurt scored at least twice. One goal fewer conceded in each would have made 18 points, level with fourth-placed Leipzig.

2.2 → 1.5

Get goals against back to normal

Frankfurt concede 2.2 a game. Their average after 9 games in past seasons is 1.5. Cutting back to that level, through a more compact block and fewer open transitions, is the single biggest lever.

1.60

Set the pace target for Europe

To reach the 52 points that sixth place usually needs, Frankfurt must take 40 from the last 25 games: 1.6 a game, up from 1.33. That is about one extra win every four games.

The good news for the club: the attack works (15 goals, in line with past seasons), and history says this start ends in mid-table on average, not in a relegation fight. Only 3 of 29 teams with 12 points after 9 finished 16th or lower.
