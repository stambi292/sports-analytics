Method and sources

  • Revenue, wages, profit: Manchester United plc full-year results releases and SEC filings, 2013/14–2024/25 (2017/18 restated for IFRS 15). 2025/26: Stock Analysis segment data, SportsCal and SI (wages reported in dollars, about £302m).
  • League finish: computed from every Premier League result 2013/14–2025/26 (football-data.co.uk).
  • The quote: The Athletic via SI, 5–6 Nov 2026; United's position at the time: The People's Person.
  • adidas clause: £10m deduction per season without Champions League from 2025/26, from the 2024/25 annual report (The Esk).
  • Rivals: commercial growth from club accounts as compiled by Glazernomics. Money League: Deloitte via Sky Sports. Inflation: ONS CPI.
