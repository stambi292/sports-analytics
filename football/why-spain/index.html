Method and sources

  • Results: every men's international since 1872 from the open international results dataset (to 6 October 2026), plus Czech Republic 0–2 Spain on 12 November 2026 (beIN Sports).
  • Elo: the eloratings.net method. Weight 60 for World Cup games, 50 for continental finals, 40 for qualifiers and Nations League, 20 for friendlies; 100 points home advantage; bigger wins move ratings more. All teams start at 1500 in 1872. This is my own model, so the numbers differ slightly from eloratings.net.
  • Unbeaten runs: a penalty shoot-out counts as a draw, as in official records. The 2025 Nations League final, lost to Portugal on penalties, is a draw.
  • Trophies: ESPN on the 2026 World Cup final; record unbeaten run: beIN Sports.
