The 115What could the charges cost Manchester City?

In September an independent commission reportedly found City guilty on 114 of 115 charges. No punishment has been set and City have appealed. The numbers show what is at stake: in titles, in this season's table, and against what one breach cost other clubs.

Stephen tAMBI · data to 12 November 2026

What City were charged with

115 charges in five groups. Almost half are about inaccurate financial information. The second biggest group is not about money at all: failing to cooperate with the investigation.

Charge groups as announced by the Premier League in February 2023. The verdict was reported by The Athletic; the Premier League has not commented, and it is not known which single charge was not proven.

What one breach has cost others

Financial cases have already cost clubs points. Each of these was for a single case, not a hundred.

Points deducted. Everton's two deductions in 2023/24 totalled 8 points. Juventus is Serie A; the others are Premier League.

Method and sources

  • Season tables 2009/10–2025/26: rebuilt from every match result (football-data.co.uk, via the datasets/football-datasets repository). Ranking by points, then goal difference, then goals scored.
  • Deduction needed: the smallest whole number of points that, taken from City alone, moves City below the given place in the final table.
  • 2026/27 table: points after 10 games, 12 November 2026.
  • Charges: Premier League announcement, February 2023, as summarised by Sports Illustrated and LBC. Verdict: reported by The Athletic, September 2026 (Al Jazeera). Appeal to be heard by January (Al Jazeera).
  • Precedents: Everton 10 points cut to 6 on appeal (Sky Sports); Juventus 10 points, May 2023 (Eurosport); Nottingham Forest 4 points and Everton's second 2 points, 2023/24.
