Can data beat the bookmaker?I built two prediction models and tested them against Bet365 on 20 seasons of Premier League football.

Every match was predicted using only what was known before kick-off. Then I compared the predictions with the bookmaker's odds, and checked what would have happened with £10 on every pick.

How the test works

1Two models

A goals model rates each team's attack and defence from recent scores. An Elo model rates teams from results, like chess ratings.

2No looking ahead

Before every match day the models are rebuilt using only earlier games. Each prediction is what you could have known that morning.

3Same question, same scoring

Everyone gives a chance for home win, draw and away win. The Brier score measures how far those chances were from what happened. Lower is better.

The scoreboard

Bookmaker chances are Bet365's odds with the margin taken out. "Picked the right result" means the outcome given the highest chance actually happened. "Guessing" uses only how often home wins, draws and away wins happened before 2006.

Method and sources

  • Data: every Premier League match 2000/01–2026/27 with Bet365 odds and best market odds, from Football-Data.co.uk via the Club Football Match Data collection (MIT licence). Test window: 2006/07–2025/26, 7,600 matches. Earlier seasons are used only to warm the models up.
  • Goals model: Poisson regression with an attack and defence rating for every team plus home advantage, fitted on the previous three years. Older matches count less (weight halves every 240 days), and a small penalty keeps promoted teams close to average. Refit before every match date. Scorelines up to 10–10 give home, draw and away chances.
  • Elo model: K = 20, home advantage 60 points, bigger wins move ratings more, 20% pull back to average each summer. An ordered logistic model, fitted only on earlier seasons, turns the rating gap into home, draw and away chances.
  • Bookmaker chances: 1 ÷ odds, scaled so the three add up to 100%. Brier score = sum of squared differences between the three chances and what happened (0 = perfect).
  • Betting: one bet per match at most, on the outcome with the biggest expected value, settled at Bet365 or best market odds. No staking tricks, no hindsight.
